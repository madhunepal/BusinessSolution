# Product Overview

## Vision

SBMS is a small-business operating system that connects customer relationships, sales, work execution, scheduling, money, inventory, people, and business activity history.

## Primary User

Small business owner or manager with 1–50 employees.

## Initial Business Model

Service-oriented businesses where the main workflow is:

Customer → Quote → Order → Job → Schedule → Complete → Invoice → Payment

## Product Principles

1. Simple enough for a small business.
2. Strong enough for operational accountability.
3. Modular enough to expand.
4. Transactional and auditable.
5. Mobile-friendly where operational workflows require it.
6. Automation-ready.
7. AI-ready only after reliable structured data exists.

## Non-Goals for V1

- full general ledger/accounting replacement
- full payroll replacement
- enterprise HR suite
- microservices architecture
- advanced AI assistant
- complex marketplace functionality

Integrate with specialized products later where appropriate.
