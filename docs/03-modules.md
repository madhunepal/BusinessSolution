# Module Map

## 1. Identity & Administration

Owns:

- users
- roles
- permissions
- business users
- application settings
- audit/security administration

## 2. Business / Organization

Owns:

- business profile
- business settings
- addresses
- tax/business metadata

## 3. CRM

Owns:

- customers
- contacts
- leads
- customer notes
- customer timeline/activity views

## 4. Sales

Owns:

- products/services used for selling
- quotes
- quote items
- sales orders
- sales order items

## 5. Jobs & Operations

Owns:

- jobs
- job tasks
- job status
- assignments
- completion workflow

## 6. Scheduling

Owns:

- appointments
- employee scheduling
- availability
- reminders

## 7. Finance

Owns:

- invoices
- invoice items
- payments
- expenses
- accounts receivable
- accounts payable

V1 should not attempt to replace a full accounting ledger.

## 8. Inventory

Owns:

- products
- stock levels
- inventory transactions
- adjustments
- reorder rules

## 9. Purchasing

Owns:

- vendors
- purchase orders
- purchase order items
- receiving

## 10. Employees

Owns:

- employee profiles
- work assignments
- time entries
- basic availability

## 11. Documents

Cross-cutting capability for storing/associating files with business records.

## 12. Notifications

Cross-cutting capability for in-app/email/SMS notifications.

## 13. Reporting

Read-oriented aggregation over approved module data.

Reporting must not become a place where transactional business rules are implemented.
